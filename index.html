Walk through it in VR on Meta Quest 3S

  1. Put on the headset, open the Meta Quest Browser and go to jagapathibanoth6-dotcom.github.io/Future-house.
  2. Tap the big 🥽 Enter VR button on the model. You start outside on the driveway at real size.
  3. Left stick walk (walk onto the stairs to climb up or down) · Right stick left/right to turn, push up or pull down to fly up or down (A, B or X lands you back on a floor) · Doors open by themselves as you walk up to them and close behind you · Trigger point at a door to open or close it yourself · Y open or close all doors · A / B next / previous room · X back outside.
  4. The theatre film (a 10-second old-film countdown with its own music) starts from the beginning when you walk into the theatre and stops when you leave.
  5. Walk around your play area for real, too: one step in your room is one step in the house.
