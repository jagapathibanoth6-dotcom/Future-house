# Future house — Parvathagiri, Warangal

3D and VR model of our G+1 house (36′ × 40′, about 2,640 sq ft built-up), with the floor plans and budget.

**Open the model:** https://jagapathibanoth6-dotcom.github.io/future-house/
**Floor plans:** https://jagapathibanoth6-dotcom.github.io/future-house/plan.html

## Walk through it in VR (Meta Quest 3S)

1. Open the model link in the **Meta Quest Browser**.
2. Tap **Enter VR**. You start outside on the driveway, at real size.
3. Controls:
   - **Left stick** – walk
   - **Right stick** left/right – turn
   - **A / B** – next / previous room
   - **X** – back outside
   - **Y** – open or close the theatre door
4. One real step in your play area is one step in the house.

The theatre screen plays a short welcome clip; use **Play my video on the theatre screen** (before entering VR) to show your own video.

## Files

- `index.html` – detailed 3D / VR model
- `plan.html` – 2D floor plans, site plan and budget calculator
